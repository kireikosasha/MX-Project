the number shown as a sum; 3 parts: a circle of 2; a circle of 5 under a guard; a row of 5 flags; 59 bits
    part 0, a circle of 2. reverse: +1
    part 1, a circle of 5. spin, only while part 0 is zero: +1; spin, only while part 0 is above zero: -1
    part 2, a row of 5 flags, one for each value of part 1. board: raise; alight: lower the flag where part 1 points
    spin: the display stays
    reverse: the display stays
    board: the flag of part 2 where part 1 points is down -> +1; otherwise the display stays
    alight: the flag of part 2 where part 1 points is up -> -1; otherwise the display stays
