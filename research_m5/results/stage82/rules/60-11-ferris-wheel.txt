the number shown as a sum; 2 parts: a circle of 4; a row of 4 flags; 36 bits
    part 0, a circle of 4. turn: +1
    part 1, a row of 4 flags, one for each value of part 0. board: raise; exit: lower the flag where part 0 points
    turn: the display stays
    board: the flag of part 1 where part 0 points is down -> +1; otherwise the display stays
    exit: the flag of part 1 where part 0 points is up -> -1; otherwise the display stays
