Why none of the 30 models of TLS-Janssen2021 was found in stages 77 and 78 (measured after those runs, on models seen).

The models. All 30 have one state every move stays in (a closed connection), and nearly every move of every other
state falls into it: of the 55 to 99 moves from the other states, 4 to 16 do not (the handshake), and the deepest
state is 4 to 6 moves from the start. A story begun at random is in the closed state after one or two symbols.

The questions (tls-1.1-openssl-1.0.2, 10 states, 11 inputs; the code and the flags of stage 78, 200 questions):
  179 questions asked before the check; all of them stories written against a second machine
   39 of them made a move not heard before, counted up to the move that closes the connection
  140 of them (78 %) closed the connection by a move already heard, and went on inside the closed state
  of the 99 moves from the 9 other states (one way to each state), 25 were known from the 300 stories taught on
  and 67 at the end; two states were never reached.
A second machine is free to differ inside the closed state (after some moves there, something else), there are
endlessly many of them, and every one costs a question.

What was tried (flags --rare --alike; two models seen, tls-1.1-mbedtls-1.2.1 of 6 states and the one above):
  the pairs of moves ordered by how seldom the place they lead to was visited: no effect, the pairs come after the
    second machines, and the questions are spent by then
  the places of the kept machine seen fewer than 22 times, before any second machine: 64 and 158 questions on the
    places of machines that were wrong; 5 states found of 10, and 5 of 6
  every symbol after a beginning that ends in an answer seen fewer than 22 times (no machine needed): the handshake
    of the 6-state model is walked in 31 questions; then the questions go to the moves that close the connection
    with a rare answer (an alert), one after another
  one beginning for a parent and an answer, a second only after all the first: 6 and 10 states, the right numbers,
    but a machine with a cell is kept that is wrong inside the closed state
  and, for the plain tables, of the machines that explain the stories the one where the fewest moves with one
    answer lead apart, the plain tables before those with a cell: both models are found exactly, 195 questions each,
    34 and 33 seconds.
